FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about appointments, insurance, and our clinical processes.

What insurances do you accept?

We accept Medicare and most major commercial insurance plans including Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, Cigna, and UnitedHealthcare. Please contact our billing coordinator prior to your first appointment for verification.

Do I need a referral to schedule an appointment?

If your insurance plan is an HMO, you will likely need a referral from your Primary Care Physician. For PPO plans, a referral is typically not required. However, we do ask for your relevant medical records to ensure comprehensive care.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring your photo ID, insurance card, a list of current medications, any recent lab results or imaging reports (X-rays, MRIs), and your completed patient intake forms.
