Clinical Diagnostic Profile

Small Joints: Hands, Wrists & Fingers

Symmetric swelling, early morning stiffness persisting over 45 minutes, and MCP/PIP joint tenderness are hallmarks of early Rheumatoid Arthritis or Psoriatic Arthritis. Early ultrasound diagnostics prevent irreversible joint erosions.

Common Manifestations:

Morning Stiffness > 45m Symmetric Swelling Grip Weakness Tenosynovitis
Recommended Rheumora Assessment: Targeted High-Resolution Musculoskeletal Ultrasound & Anti-CCP Serology
