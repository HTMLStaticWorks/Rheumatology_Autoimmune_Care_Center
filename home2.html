Clinical Diagnostic Profile

Small Joints: Hands, Wrists & Fingers

Symmetric swelling, early morning stiffness persisting over 45 minutes, and MCP/PIP joint tenderness are hallmarks of early Rheumatoid Arthritis or Psoriatic Arthritis. Early ultrasound diagnostics prevent irreversible joint erosions.

Common Manifestations:

Morning Stiffness > 45m Symmetric Swelling Grip Weakness Tenosynovitis
Recommended Rheumora Assessment: Targeted High-Resolution Musculoskeletal Ultrasound & Anti-CCP Serology

The Rheumora Care Journey

Your Pathway from Acute Flare to Remission

A structured, 4-phase clinical methodology ensuring you never navigate your health journey without proactive guidance.

PHASE 01

Comprehensive Intake

60-minute physician consultation, point-of-care ultrasound, and exhaustive autoimmune serology panels.

PHASE 02

Precision Targeting

Selection of targeted biologic therapies, oral DMARDs, and immediate pain-relief interventions.

PHASE 03

Infusion Tranquility

On-site therapeutic biologic administration in dedicated suites monitored by rheumatology nurse specialists.

PHASE 04

Remission Monitoring

Regular biomarker reassessment, ultrasound joint tracking, and personalized lifestyle adjustments.
