Clinical Specialties & Infusion Services

Every autoimmune condition presents differently. Our specialized clinics deliver targeted biologic therapies, precise imaging, and compassionate multidisciplinary support.

Early Synovial Care

Rheumatoid Arthritis Clinic

Targeted early suppression of synovial inflammation using next-generation targeted synthetic DMARDs (JAK inhibitors) and biologic TNF/IL-6 inhibitors. Our objective is strict clinical remission according to ACR/EULAR criteria.

Point-of-care joint ultrasound included

Biologic Infusion Suites

Experience therapeutic intravenous infusions in private or semi-private bays featuring heated massage recliners, entertainment tablets, and dedicated 1-on-1 certified rheumatology infusion nursing.

Connective Tissue Care

Systemic Lupus and Sjogren's

Coordinated connective tissue management focusing on renal protection, anti-malarial optimization (hydroxychloroquine monitoring), B-lymphocyte stimulator inhibitors (Belimumab), and fatigue mitigation.

Multi-organ screening & complement labs
Book Evaluation Enquire
Spine & Peripheral Joints

Psoriatic Arthritis & Spondyloarthritis

Specialized treatment for enthesitis, dactylitis ("sausage fingers"), and axial sacroiliitis. We leverage IL-17 and IL-23 pathway inhibitors to simultaneously clear cutaneous plaques and arrest spinal stiffness.

Joint & skin dual-endpoint monitoring

Point-of-Care Imaging

Musculoskeletal Ultrasound (MSUS)

High-frequency power Doppler ultrasound uncovers active hypervascular inflammation and cartilage erosions months before standard X-rays can detect changes. Also used for pinpoint-accurate aspiration and injections.

Immediate real-time visualization
Book Joint Scan Enquire
Skeletal Bone Health

Bone Mineral & Osteoporosis Clinic

Evaluation for steroid-induced or post-menopausal bone density loss. We administer advanced bone-building anabolic agents (Teriparatide, Romosozumab) and antiresorptive therapies to safeguard skeletal resilience.

Comprehensive FRAX risk modeling
